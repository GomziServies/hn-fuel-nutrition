The true strength of HN Fuel :

HN Fuel Whey Protein is a whey protein blend with 24g protein per serving, 5.71g BCAAs, and 11.86g EAAs. It is designed to support muscle growth, recovery, and everyday nutrition for active lifestyles.

How to Use:

Add 1 scoop in 220-250ml of water.

Shake & stir until it dissolves completely.

Shake is ready to be consumed.

Ingredients:

Whey Protein blend (Whey Protein Concentrate, Whey Protein Isolate), Milk Solids, Mango Pulp Powder, Artificial Flavouring Substance (Mango), Emulsifier [INS 322(i)], Stabilizer [INS 415], Digestive Enzymes, Sweetener [INS 955].

Allergen Advice:

This product contains lactose and is not recommended for those with lactose intolerance.

Taste Advice:

For a thick, creamy, and delicious shake, use non-fat or skimmed milk. Nuts can be added as per your calorie requirements.

Recommended Duration of Use:

Use this product for at least 30 days for effective results, or as advised by your healthcare professional.

Recommended Use:

TWO SERVINGS PER DAY.

Storage Instructions:

Store in a cool & dry place. Keep away from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children.

Caution:

Consult a healthcare practitioner especially in pregnancy, liver disease or if advised to follow a low-protein diet. Do not exceed the recommended daily intake.
